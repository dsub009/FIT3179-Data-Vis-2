2. What's on the shelf?

Snacks and sweets are the clear outlier. Half of them have more than 39.7 g of sugar per 100 g, while pasta, rice and grains have a median of just 0.6 g.

Star ratings follow the same pattern. 11 of the 18 snacks and sweets earn only half a star, while all 20 pasta, rice and grain products earn 3 stars or more.

Source: OpenHSR (N'kam Suguem and Lafargue, 2025).
